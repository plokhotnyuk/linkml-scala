## Inherited fields

A class extends the abstract classes and traits of its parent classes and mixins, which declare its fields as `def`s. A parent leaves out the `def` when a descendant gives the field a type that can't override it, such as with a `slot_usage` that makes it required. See [Slots changed by a class](/docs/slot-usage-inheritance/#slots-changed-by-a-class).
